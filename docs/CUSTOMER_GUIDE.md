# Tex-Trak: Receiving your deliveries

Tex-Trak lets you see deliveries on their way to you and accept them when they arrive. Your acceptance, your name and your signature are recorded, and the sender sees the result straight away.

Support: **surya@circai.co.uk**

---

## The easy way: the link in your email

When a delivery is booked for you, the sender sends you a link by email or message. **Open it on your phone. There is nothing to sign in to.** The page shows:

- **Before it arrives:** a live map of where your delivery is.
- **When it arrives:** the page changes by itself, with a notification, to **Confirm your delivery** at the top of the screen. Choose *All items arrived* (or enter a different number), type your name, sign with your finger and press the big **Accept delivery** button. That is all.
- **If you want to scan:** choose *Scan the items* and scan each label instead.

Keep the message. The same link works until the delivery is confirmed, and afterwards it shows your confirmation. Anyone with the link can confirm, so please do not forward it.

The rest of this guide is for customers who also have a Tex-Trak sign-in.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the Tex-Trak address you were given.
2. Enter the **username** and **password** you were sent. Press *Show* to check what you typed.
3. You land on **Your deliveries**. This is the only part of Tex-Trak you need.

You can also use the Tex-Trak app on an Android phone (choose **Download the mobile app** in the left menu). In **My account** you can set a quick PIN.

---

## 2. Your deliveries

The list shows every delivery sent to you, newest first.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Booked** | The sender has prepared it. It has not left yet. |
| **On the way** | It has been dispatched. |
| **Delivered** | You accepted it and the number matched. |
| **Problem** | The number you accepted did not match what was sent. The sender has been told. |

Tap a delivery to see where it is coming from, what is in it, and its history. A notification appears at the top right when something changes.

---

## 3. Following your delivery

Once a delivery has left, open it to see a **live map**: where it is now, the route it has taken, and the area around where it is going. The map refreshes by itself every 15 seconds. You cannot sign for it yet.

When it reaches you, Tex-Trak notices by itself, a notification appears, and the page changes to **Accept this delivery**. Until then you can only follow it.

## 4. Accepting a delivery

When your delivery has arrived, open it and look for the blue **Accept this delivery** box. Choose **one** of two ways to check it.

### Option A: Scan the items
Use this when each item has a QR label.
1. Choose **Scan the items**.
2. Press **Start camera** and point it at each label, or use a scanner, or type the code.
3. Each good scan makes **one short beep** and the counter goes up (for example *12 of 20 scanned*).
4. A code that does not belong to this delivery, or one you have already scanned, makes a **lower double buzz** and a message.
5. When you have finished, check the counter. Anything not scanned is reported as missing, so scan again if you have only missed one.

### Option B: Confirm without scanning
Use this when you have checked the items yourself.
1. Choose **Confirm without scanning**.
2. Check **How many items did you receive?** It starts with the number that was sent. Change it if fewer arrived.
3. Tick the confirmation: *I have scanned and checked the items. The quantity and the items received are correct.*

### Then, for both options
1. Type the **name of the person receiving**.
2. **Sign** in the box with your finger or a mouse. Press *Clear signature* to start again.
3. Add a **note** if useful, for example *one box damaged*.
4. Press **Accept delivery**.

The delivery is marked **Delivered** if the number matches, or **Problem** if it does not. Your name, signature, time and method (scanned or confirmed) are saved with it.

---

## 5. If something is wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A delivery is missing from the list | It has not been booked to your account. Ask the sender. |
| Fewer items arrived | Scan only what is there, or enter the lower number, add a note, sign and accept. The sender is told straight away. |
| The camera does not start | Allow camera access in your phone or browser settings, or type the code. |
| A code is rejected | It is not part of this delivery. Check you scanned the right label. |
| The signature box will not accept a signature | Press *Clear signature* and sign again, drawing a line in the box. |
| Cannot sign in | Check the username and password. Contact the sender or support if your account has been switched off. |
